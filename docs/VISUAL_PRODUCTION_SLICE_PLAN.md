# Visual Production Slice Plan — Visual Replacement Production V2

Primary visual authority: approved GRAVIVORE prototype board `Концепт GRAVIVORE: Мехи и окружение.png`.

## 1. Goal

Do not replace Chapter 1 wholesale before proving that the target art direction survives the real gameplay camera, mobile readability and runtime constraints.

The first production slice is a **single representative combat area** that proves player, ordinary enemies, elite hierarchy, environment density, materials, lighting, attack readability and death readability together.

## 2. Exact first slice

Build exactly this first:

- G-0 Tier 0;
- Scout;
- Cutter as the second ordinary enemy;
- Magnetar Guard as the elite;
- one industrial gameplay area approximately 15 x 20 meters;
- reusable floor kit;
- reusable wall/barrier kit;
- one hero reactor;
- 4–6 supporting props;
- one authored mechanical gate;
- production-intent lighting pass;
- one representative G-0 attack context;
- one Scout/Cutter death context;
- one Magnetar attack context.

Why Magnetar before Custodian: the elite is large enough to test escalation, armor complexity and camera crowding without spending boss-level production time before the art language is proven.

## 3. Slice layout

The 15 x 20 m area should contain:

### Combat center
- roughly 9 x 12 m of readable movement space;
- floor detail through seams, grates, insets and wear rather than collision clutter;
- one low barrier or machinery interruption to test occlusion/readability.

### One long edge
- layered wall family;
- integrated gate;
- pipe/conduit routing;
- warm practical light.

### Opposite edge
- hero reactor cluster;
- two supporting machines/props;
- stronger warm orange energy source.

### Remaining edges
- low barrier, tanks/containers, maintenance object and one cyan service-tech accent.

The area must look functional from the first screenshot. No placeholder cubes may remain in the acceptance capture.

## 4. Required environment kit for evaluation

Before evaluation, the slice must include at minimum:

### Floors
- 3 distinct 2x2 or 4x4 base patterns;
- 1 grate/service variant;
- 1 damaged/worn variant;
- edge/corner trim.

### Walls / barriers
- 2 wall bay variants;
- 1 corner;
- 1 half-height barrier;
- 1 pipe-integrated wall variant;
- authored top/side termination so modules do not look cut off.

### Gate
- complete frame;
- visible actuator/locking language;
- threshold/floor track;
- open and closed visual state compatibility.

### Hero reactor
- layered frame;
- visible energy chamber;
- pipe/cable connections;
- maintenance logic;
- enough silhouette detail to act as landmark.

### Supporting props
Choose 4–6 from:
- vertical tank;
- horizontal tank;
- machine block/pump;
- maintenance console;
- cable junction;
- tool/service cart;
- column/pylon;
- storage crate family.

At least four must have distinct silhouettes.

## 5. Character production minimum

### G-0 Tier 0
Must include:
- final-intent silhouette;
- production topology suitable for rigging;
- core housing;
- layered armor;
- visible joint mechanics;
- player material set;
- attack/VFX sockets;
- rig or articulation solution sufficient for idle/locomotion/attack proof.

### Scout
Must include:
- final-intent small spider/radial silhouette;
- low-cost articulation;
- red core;
- melee attack pose/read;
- death breakup/animation compatibility.

### Cutter
Must include:
- distinct narrow blade silhouette;
- attack-ready blade articulation;
- visible core/body;
- no silhouette confusion with Scout.

### Magnetar Guard
Must include:
- elite-scale body;
- heavy layered armor;
- broader multi-leg stance;
- unique attack geometry;
- amber/orange energy containment;
- one readable weak/focal area.

## 6. Material production minimum

No untextured gray-box geometry in final slice review.

Required:
- graphite structural material family;
- cool player armor;
- hostile dark/gray armor;
- elite warm accent material;
- mid-tone environment metal;
- hazard coating;
- cyan emission;
- warm orange emission;
- enemy red emission;
- wear/dirt treatment;
- normal/roughness response visible under scene lighting.

## 7. Lighting production minimum

The slice must prove:
- warm industrial practical lighting;
- cool player/service separation;
- readable dark structure;
- readable floor boundaries;
- enemy silhouettes in both warm and neutral zones;
- no dependency on extreme bloom;
- no permanent light that washes out attack telegraphs.

## 8. Attack context proof

### G-0 attack
At least one attack shown from the real gameplay camera with:
- visible weapon origin;
- attack direction readable before impact;
- cyan effect separated from body core;
- effect not hiding G-0 silhouette.

### Magnetar attack
At least one elite attack with:
- pose/geometry telegraph;
- warm/orange charge language;
- readable area or direction;
- player remains visible during effect.

## 9. Death context proof

Scout or Cutter death must show modern readable visual language:
- immediate hit confirmation;
- short mechanical collapse/break/depower response;
- core/emission shutdown or disruption;
- restrained sparks/fragments;
- no 2000s-style generic explosion replacing all body response.

The body may dissolve/despawn later, but the first death beat must be physical and readable.

## 10. Evaluation captures

Acceptance requires real gameplay-camera evidence, not beauty renders only.

Minimum capture set:
1. neutral gameplay frame with G-0 + Scout + Cutter;
2. same area with Magnetar present;
3. G-0 attack frame;
4. enemy attack frame;
5. death frame;
6. gate/reactor environment-only frame from gameplay camera;
7. phone-size composite or screenshots at target portrait resolution;
8. one close-up material/model frame for G-0;
9. one close-up material/model frame for environment kit.

## 11. Acceptance grades

### A — scale production
- clearly belongs to approved prototype language;
- no cubic/toy-like impression;
- silhouettes read immediately;
- G-0 feels distinctive and premium enough for external testers;
- Magnetar unmistakably elite;
- environment feels authored and functional;
- materials/lighting hold up on phone;
- attack/death language feels modern;
- mobile budgets are credible.

Result: approved to scale across Chapter 1.

### B — acceptable with targeted fixes
- visual identity is correct;
- main silhouettes and environment quality are convincing;
- 2–4 contained issues remain, such as one weak prop family, material tuning, a specific attack effect, or a detail-density mismatch;
- no fundamental redesign required.

Result: fix listed issues, then scale.

### C — internal proof only
- some art is improved but still visibly prototype-like;
- one or more hero assets feel generic/toy-like;
- environment still reads as test scene or obvious kitbash;
- elite hierarchy weak;
- phone readability inconsistent;
- material/lighting language not coherent.

Result: **do not scale**. Rework slice.

### Reject
Any of:
- cubic G-0/enemies;
- primitive gate/walls;
- empty arena;
- generic sci-fi with no prototype identity;
- major silhouette confusion;
- close-up-only quality;
- excessive neon;
- model/prop imports with no coherent art pass;
- unacceptable mobile cost;
- attack/death presentation remains visibly outdated.

Result: stop production and redesign.

## 12. Non-negotiable gate

Anything graded **C or Reject must not be propagated to the rest of Chapter 1**. The slice exists specifically to prevent expensive replication of weak art direction.
