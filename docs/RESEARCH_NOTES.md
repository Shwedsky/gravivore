# Research notes — baseline

Checked: 2026-09-24.

## Butcher Hero / formula

The project is based on genre-level observations only. No Butcher Hero code/assets are to be extracted.

Observed product pattern:
- one-thumb portrait movement;
- auto engagement;
- permanent stat growth from prey;
- increasingly strong enemies/bosses;
- equipment and layered metagame in the mature product;
- AFK/auto progression features in later versions;
- monetization through store products/passes/ads.

Related market references:
- Butcher Hero
- Alien Invasion
- Devour Idle RPG
- XP Hero

Use these as design comparison points, not implementation sources.

## RuStore payment screenshots

The supplied screenshots visibly show:
- RuStore-branded payment sheet;
- product `Small Gems Pack`;
- price 99 RUB;
- payment methods including SBP;
- separate bank selector;
- T-Bank and Alfa-Bank app choices.

Official RuStore documentation confirms this interaction model: SBP payment selection can open a bank chooser and redirect to the installed bank app; deeplinks handle return to the game.

## Name check

`CoreVore` is already used by a separate Steam game as of 2026-09-24, therefore do not use it as the public title.

`GRAVIVORE` is the project codename. Public naming/trademark clearance remains a pre-release business task.
