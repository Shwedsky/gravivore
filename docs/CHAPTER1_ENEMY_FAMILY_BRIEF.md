# Chapter 1 Enemy Family Brief — Visual Replacement Production V2

Primary visual authority: approved GRAVIVORE prototype board `Концепт GRAVIVORE: Мехи и окружение.png`.

## 1. Family rule

Chapter 1 enemies must look like related industrial war-machines without collapsing into the same body with different weapons. The prototype establishes five ordinary roles with immediate silhouette contrast, one clearly stronger elite, and one arena-defining boss.

Recognition target: each ordinary enemy must be identifiable in **under one second** from the real portrait gameplay camera, even if all emissive colors are mentally desaturated.

Shared family traits:
- dark mechanical structure;
- hostile red/orange energy language except the role-specific Arc Drone;
- layered hard-surface shells;
- tapered limbs or appendages;
- visible energy core or attack focal point;
- industrial rather than military-human design vocabulary.

## 2. Scout

### Gameplay role
Fast melee pressure unit.

### Size vs G-0
~0.55–0.70× G-0 perceived mass.

### Silhouette
Small radial/spider body, thin quick legs, round/compact central body, strong visible red core. Low and wide.

### Center of mass
Very low, centered directly above the core body.

### Locomotion idea
Fast multi-legged scuttle with quick lateral changes. Small vertical body bob; legs carry most motion.

### Weapon / attack language
Short-range pounce, claw/jab, or contact strike. Attack geometry should be compact and fast rather than long-bladed.

### Armor / material language
Thin dark shell plates, red hostile core, less plating than G-0. More exposed mechanics around legs.

### Emissive accents
Red core plus tiny limb-root status accents only.

### Weak point / readability
Core is the obvious focal target.

### Visual danger level
Low individually, threatening in groups.

### Distinction
Smallest ground enemy, thinnest legs, most radial silhouette, no shield, no long blades, no heavy cargo mass.

## 3. Cutter

### Gameplay role
High-damage melee striker.

### Size vs G-0
~0.75–0.95× G-0 perceived mass.

### Silhouette
Narrow, front-heavy predator with one or two conspicuous tapered cutting structures. More directional and asymmetrical than Scout.

### Center of mass
Low-to-mid, shifted slightly rearward to visually balance long front blades.

### Locomotion idea
Fast committed lunges, skating/scuttling acceleration, decisive stopping posture.

### Weapon / attack language
Visible cutting arcs, lunges, crossing blade strikes. Weapon silhouette must read before attack VFX appears.

### Armor / material language
Dark hostile body with gray armor sections and red/orange weapon/core energy. Blade surfaces should be metallic, not pure emissive planes.

### Emissive accents
Red core and narrow blade-root / power-channel accents.

### Weak point / readability
Central body/core remains readable behind blade geometry.

### Visual danger level
Medium-high burst threat.

### Distinction
Longest ordinary melee reach; strongest blade language; narrower than Warden and Carrier; less radial than Scout.

## 4. Arc Drone

### Gameplay role
Ranged energy attacker.

### Size vs G-0
~0.45–0.65× G-0 body mass, elevated by hover height.

### Silhouette
Floating radial/tri-wing construct with a bright circular center and three/four angular fins or emitters. No ground-contact legs.

### Center of mass
Exactly around glowing central energy sphere/lens.

### Locomotion idea
Hover, smooth strafing, small rotational tracking, controlled vertical oscillation.

### Weapon / attack language
Charged energy bolt, arc beam, or radial discharge. Fins may align or open during charge.

### Armor / material language
Dark metallic fins around a brighter energy assembly. Keep body compact; avoid generic spherical drone simplicity.

### Emissive accents
Blue/cyan role energy is acceptable because prototype uses blue. Hostility must be communicated by shape, attack behavior and darker hostile housing so it cannot be confused with G-0.

### Weak point / readability
Central orb/core is the obvious target and attack origin.

### Visual danger level
Medium, spatial pressure from range.

### Distinction
Only fully hovering ordinary unit; radial fin silhouette; circular ranged energy center.

## 5. Warden / Shield type

### Gameplay role
Armored slow blocker / shield enemy.

### Size vs G-0
~0.95–1.15× perceived mass.

### Silhouette
Broad frontal slab/shield mass attached to a compact heavy body. Low mobility stance, short thick supports.

### Center of mass
Low and forward behind shield.

### Locomotion idea
Deliberate heavy steps, braced pauses, small recoil under impact.

### Weapon / attack language
Shield shove, short-range slam, defensive facing. May have secondary compact weapon but shield is primary read.

### Armor / material language
Highest armor coverage among ordinary enemies. Thick gray/dark shell, reinforced frame, limited exposed mechanism.

### Emissive accents
Small orange/red core and narrow status lights; do not overpower shield silhouette.

### Weak point / readability
Weak/rear/side energy zone or core exposure when opening attack posture.

### Visual danger level
Medium due to durability and lane blocking.

### Distinction
Widest defensive front, shortest visible weapon reach, most armored ordinary body.

## 6. Carrier / Hauler type

### Gameplay role
Heavy support / hauler / reinforcement platform.

### Size vs G-0
~1.15–1.35× perceived mass.

### Silhouette
Tallest/heaviest ordinary unit with thick multi-jointed supports and a bulky equipment-bearing torso. Prototype reads orange/gray and industrial.

### Center of mass
Mid-high compared with other ordinary enemies, supported by a broad stance.

### Locomotion idea
Heavy measured steps. Slight chassis inertia. Secondary compartments or equipment may react during attacks/support actions.

### Weapon / attack language
Support pulse, deployable payload, heavy ranged burst, or reinforcement action depending gameplay. Avoid giving it Cutter-like blades.

### Armor / material language
Industrial hauler panels, reinforced orange/dark armor, visible utility modules or cargo-like machinery integrated into the silhouette.

### Emissive accents
Orange/red internal energy and service lights.

### Weak point / readability
Large power assembly or exposed support module distinct from the armored carrying body.

### Visual danger level
Medium-high strategic threat.

### Distinction
Heaviest ordinary silhouette, most vertical/mechanical equipment mass, thickest legs after elite/boss.

## 7. Magnetar Guard — Elite

### Gameplay role
Progression check / elite combat encounter.

### Size vs G-0
~1.5–1.9× perceived mass.

### Silhouette
Prototype shows a significantly larger, broader multi-legged mech with heavy layered armor, thick supports and orange energy. Must look like an advanced guardian, not a recolored Carrier.

### Center of mass
Dense and stable, slightly elevated above ordinary enemies with strong shoulder/lateral armor masses.

### Locomotion idea
Heavy but purposeful multi-leg movement. Short power bursts or magnetic/energy attack postures may temporarily widen the silhouette.

### Weapon / attack language
Unique elite attacks: magnetic containment, heavy sweeping strike, energy pulse, or strong area denial. Weapons should appear integrated into armor/limb architecture rather than held like guns.

### Armor / material language
More plates, deeper overlaps, larger joint housings, darker structural internals, warm orange/amber energy. Use stronger material contrast than ordinary enemies.

### Emissive accents
Orange/amber core or containment nodes; localized but more numerous than ordinary enemies.

### Weak point / readability
One dominant exposed energy assembly or attack-state opening. It should be visible enough to read during real combat but not permanently unarmored.

### Visual danger level
High.

### Distinction
Elite must exceed every ordinary enemy in armor layering, stance width, perceived mechanical density and attack reach. If silhouette reads as “Carrier but 30% bigger,” reject.

## 8. Custodian M-0 — Boss

### Gameplay role
Chapter 1 final test / arena-defining boss.

### Size vs G-0
~3–4× perceived body mass with several times the limb span.

### Silhouette
Huge spider-like industrial guardian with many long articulated legs/arms and a massive circular red core in the torso. Prototype establishes extreme width and limb reach.

### Center of mass
Large central armored body suspended above a wide multi-leg support footprint.

### Locomotion idea
Deliberate heavy repositioning, planted attack stances, long limb articulation. Boss should feel structurally massive even when moving quickly for attacks.

### Weapon / attack language
Large readable attack families: sweeping limb attacks, targeted beam/laser, radial or ground telegraph strikes. Physical geometry and attack pose must telegraph before VFX.

### Armor / material language
Deep dark/red industrial armor, multiple shell levels, massive joint housings, visible internal red energy containment. More complex than Magnetar but organized around a few readable macro masses.

### Emissive accents
Large red/orange central core as dominant hostile focal point; secondary weapon nodes and joint/containment indicators. Avoid lighting every leg edge.

### Weak point / readability
Central core is primary visual target, with attack-state exposure or armor framing to preserve gameplay clarity.

### Visual danger level
Maximum.

### Distinction
Custodian cannot share the same footprint or body plan as ordinary enemies. It must define the arena composition and feel like the industrial system’s guardian mechanism.

## 10. Family consistency rules

MUST:
- keep ordinary units distinct in black silhouette;
- preserve shared industrial/mechanical ancestry;
- give every unit a readable attack origin;
- ensure ground-contact method is obvious;
- maintain localized energy accents;
- reserve boss-level limb span and core scale for Custodian.

MUST NOT:
- reuse the same torso and only swap weapons;
- rely on red/orange/blue color to identify classes;
- make all enemies humanoid;
- make Magnetar merely a scaled Carrier;
- make Custodian merely a scaled Magnetar;
- hide all weak points behind VFX or tiny texture details.

## 11. Gameplay-camera acceptance

For each enemy produce a neutral gameplay-camera capture at actual scale with emission reduced. A reviewer must correctly identify role from silhouette and proportion before approving materials. If two ordinary enemies can be confused in under one second, redesign the silhouette before texturing.
