# Chapter 01 concept fidelity V2

Baseline: `5999bffd0eb4aa5b291f2347aa4c2704ae98af45` (merged PR #63).

## Deliverable

One continuous playable hero route from Repair Hub through ordinary combat,
industrial transition, elite approach, Magnetar, containment approach and
Custodian. Existing Chapter 01 gameplay remains authoritative. The next owner
device gate is the final concept fidelity APK; there are no intermediate owner
art or device approvals.

## Production direction

- Graphite structure, pale beveled armor and cool steel, with rounded pressure
  housings, exposed dark mechanisms and curved rubber services.
- Irregular deck plates, interrupted maintenance seams, welded repairs,
  recessed service channels and deliberately composed damage.
- Cyan repair, amber machinery and red containment light originates inside
  recessed machinery, behind cages and overlapping shells.
- Each route segment has a large machinery focal point and authored transition
  assets. Combat lanes, gate topology and telegraphs stay readable.
- Repair Hub retains repair authority and manipulator timing while replacing
  visible temporary geometry with authored servo arms, housings and tools.
- Custodian is a new arena-defining containment machine with a layered curved
  shell, internal power chamber and articulated weapon/support mechanisms.
  Its presentation follows existing attack, hit and shutdown authority.

## Asset and verification contract

Final visible hero assets are authored in Blender and imported as production
meshes/prefabs. C# supplies behavior and integration, not runtime modelling.
Actor assets include pivots, animation clips and LOD0/1/2. Visual prefabs have
no gameplay collider or authority. Shared materials bound shader diversity.

Review at the actual portrait gameplay camera. Record route captures, asset
budgets, tests, project validation, Android build and cold-start limitations.
Do not claim concept acceptance or device performance from editor captures.
Commit and push after substantial art/runtime checkpoints.

## Preserved behavior

120-second ordinary wave cooldown; map timers; walls and gates; minimap;
repair authority; progression; health/damage/reward UI; quests; saves;
Magnetar/Custodian repeat encounters; boss UI; Russian localization; G-0 and
accepted enemy silhouettes. No balance changes or Chapter 02 work.
