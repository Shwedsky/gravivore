# S20 Fresh-Profile Playthrough Checklist

## Setup

- Install the S20 Candidate APK on the target portrait Android device.
- Clear app data or use the verified profile-reset path, then fully restart the app.
- Record device model, Android version, display aspect ratio, build version, and APK filename.
- Start one uninterrupted fresh-profile timer when control becomes available.

## Timing log

Record elapsed time for every item. Use `not reached` rather than estimating a missed event.

- Fresh profile start.
- First enemy kill.
- First permanent stat increase and which stat.
- Tier 1 visual evolution.
- Completion of all five introductory spot objectives.
- Elite gate unlock.
- Magnetar Guard kill.
- Tier 2 visual evolution.
- First Custodian M-0 attempt.
- Every player death, with context.
- Custodian M-0 kill.
- Final Power, Hull, Armor, Flux, and Mobility levels.
- Total completion time.
- Every farming segment that felt empty, forced, repetitive, or blocked by respawn.

## Qualitative checks

- Camera shows meaningfully more world than the pre-S20 build while the player and five enemy roles remain readable.
- Boss arena, boss body, and major telegraphs fit comfortably enough for portrait decisions.
- Comparable ordinary enemies proactively engage at a reasonable local distance.
- Moderately outscaled ordinary enemies use a visibly shorter acquisition distance.
- Massively outscaled ordinary enemies do not cross a whole area to chase.
- Deliberately approaching or attacking a weak enemy still starts combat.
- HP, objectives, level-up feedback, and contextual boss HP remain readable without a permanent stat list.
- Menu opens from the compact button, immediately pauses movement/combat, and resumes correctly.
- Characteristics show all five levels and useful derived values.
- Equipment shows all three current slots without implying unsupported inventory editing.
- Sound, volume, and haptics controls remain independent.
- DEV and menu controls are visually compact, have comfortable touch targets, remain inside Safe Area, and do not overlap.

## Result

Record PASS only after the whole fresh-profile run. Attach milestone times, final levels, deaths, and concise notes about grind, camera, aggro, boss readability, and menu usability.
