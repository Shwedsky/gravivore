# Chapter 01 architecture audit V1

Reviewed 2026-10-13 against main `e1669362fd3566f69939b547f263a3a600e3e1b7`, with the accepted Energy Chamber promoted into the actual Chapter. Classification concerns environment architecture, not enemy actors. This is an audit and ranked replacement plan; it does not approve the rest of the Chapter as production art.

## Method and evidence

Loaded `Assets/_Game/Content/Scenes/Chapter01_ScrapExclusion.unity`, resolved actual prefab/mesh references and measured the active hierarchy. The real scene uses Chapter01CompleteConcept assets for RepairHub, RelayYard, ShieldDump, HaulerGraveyard, MagnetarComplex, CustodianCore and ChapterServiceNetwork; CuttingFloor/IndustrialTransfer come from ConnectedConceptSectors; CapacitorField comes from CapacitorFieldProof. Similarly named BlueprintWorldR1 prefabs are not the active landmark presentations. Names alone were not used to assign quality.

Reviewed fifteen unretouched Unity renders from the unchanged production portrait lens: eleven major prefab groups and four gate/transition approaches. Each includes unchanged unit-scale G-0. The Energy Chamber also has six captures from the live Chapter and controller traversal evidence. [Inventory, camera receipts and screenshots](history/implementation-passes/chapter01-architecture-production-v1/) record exact assets and anchors. Sector review uses approach positions where the main machinery and fighting lane share the frame; it does not isolate props for beauty-shot acceptance.

Existing visual landmark prefabs own zero colliders by design. Their physicality is supplied by `BlueprintWorldR1/Chapter01WorldLayout.asset` and `Chapter01WorldPresenter.BuildLayoutVolume`, with separate boxes for walls, machinery, supports and gate authority. Existing blocker/surface definitions remain unchanged; four route waypoints are added around the new chamber's northern service passage. The chamber's explicitly referenced compound collision root is adopted under gameplay geometry during world composition, so the HardBlocker layer, wall envelopes and tactical map share the same twenty-five boxes. The visual assembly owns no runtime behavior. That separation is valid, but does not certify every fine visible part of the other facilities. The existing full-Chapter traversal smoke test checks route continuity; the new chamber also receives local controller and actual locomotion-envelope tests. Later replacements must revalidate their own mesh-to-proxy fit.

## Major landmarks

**Energy Chamber — KEEP.** Production prefab at `(11,.08,50.65)`, 45 renderers / 279,694 triangles at LOD0. Production placement is 0.65 m south of the accepted context reference, to widen the northern service passage for existing actor movement envelopes; rotation and scale are unchanged. Function reads as containment and distribution: a dominant orange chamber, layered stepped plinth, four subordinate vertical cyan nodes, generators and physically connected radial service bridges. Dark structure, shell, steel and technical materials remain distinct against G-0. The form survives the actual camera; near views preserve the accepted arrangement. Twenty-five simple boxes cover solid machinery and stairs, with two logical diagonal service pockets and accessible west/north/east circulation. The south side faces the existing closed platform rim, not a service through-route. The accepted presentation is frozen. This is the Chapter quality benchmark, not a circular-shape template.

**RepairHub — REBUILD.** Active `Chapter01CompleteConcept/Prefabs/RepairHub.prefab`, 64 renderers / 148,818 triangles. Function is suggested by the cyan floor service ring and opposing manipulators, but the primary installation is still a flat floor mark framed by large chamfered box housings. The manipulators read as simple abstract arms rather than a layered repair machine. Repeated vent plates provide detail without enough structural depth or visible tool/service connections. Scale and the central movement pocket work; cool service color and separate layout blockers should be retained. At the gameplay camera the surrounding columns dominate the identity. Build one functional maintenance installation with diagnostic/tool routing and substantial service structure around the open G-0 pocket.

**RelayYard — REBUILD.** Active `Chapter01CompleteConcept/Prefabs/RelayYard.prefab`, 50 renderers / 124,122 triangles. The open deployment mouth, rails and clear forecourt support encounter readability, but the main silhouette is a broad rectangular lintel carrying five repeated little boxes between generic posts. Side pedestals repeat the same housing language. Depth, believable roof/load structure, communication hardware and bay mechanics are insufficient for the chamber benchmark; warning color cannot supply that missing identity. Preserve spawn authority, service opening, approach width and G-0 scale while replacing the primary facility form.

**CapacitorField — REFINE.** Active `CapacitorFieldProof/Prefabs/CapacitorField.prefab`, 20 renderers / 157,430 triangles. Tall unequal energy cylinders, connected top/bottom routing, raised edge treatment and a clear ranged lane communicate distribution. The primary vertical silhouette works and contrasts with the warm fabrication sector. Bright broad cyan surfaces flatten internal depth; secondary generator cabinets remain box-heavy. Retain cylinders and layout, refine contained-energy surfaces, support assemblies, manifolds, purposeful joints and service access. Physical boxes stay external to presentation and need renewed fit checks after refinement.

**CuttingFloor — REFINE.** Active `ConnectedConceptSectors/Prefabs/CuttingFloor.prefab`, 15 renderers / 83,788 triangles. A heavy open press/work cell, paired upper cylinders, ram, stepped work bed and warm hazardous light read as fabrication before combat. Main masses, structural thickness, working depth and clear approach survive the lens. Large flat roof/side housings, simplified hydraulic connections and repeated cabinet surfaces fall below the accepted chamber detail/material hierarchy. Retain the press silhouette and combat pocket; add convincing actuator load paths, feed/service connections and restrained working-surface wear. No Cutter actor or behavior change is implied.

**ShieldDump — REBUILD.** Active `Chapter01CompleteConcept/Prefabs/ShieldDump.prefab`, 55 renderers / 130,578 triangles. Heavy storage/armor processing is conveyed mostly by chunky housings and color. The visible central processing object is a small flat rectangular box on a stepped pedestal, surrounded by repeated chamfered wall columns. It lacks a distinctive armor-handling mechanism, dimensional work cell and plausible transfer path. G-0 scale and clear fighting lane are serviceable; the silhouette and box-based primary geometry need replacement. Keep Warden-owned content and existing encounter semantics separate.

**HaulerGraveyard — REBUILD.** Active `Chapter01CompleteConcept/Prefabs/HaulerGraveyard.prefab`, 58 renderers / 113,978 triangles. Wreck/cargo silhouettes communicate logistics, but exposed rectangular tubs on simple circular wheels and folded plate fragments read as toy carts rather than industrial transport remnants. Asymmetry is present; layered hull thickness, ruptured systems, suspension/load machinery and coherent wreck connections are weak. Warm/cool accents and the open lane do not solve the primary geometry. Replace environment wrecks and loading architecture, retaining traversal proxies until revised and validated. The live Carrier actor is excluded.

**MagnetarComplex — REFINE, implementation deferred.** Active `Chapter01CompleteConcept/Prefabs/MagnetarComplex.prefab`, 65 renderers / 156,154 triangles. The broad induction bowl, nested amber cylinder, surrounding frame and lateral supply line give a recognizable elite facility and strong main mass. The upper frame is still box-like, the central cylinder smooth and underspecified, and the induction/support hardware lacks the chamber's layered functional construction. Scale and approach leave the elite fighting pocket readable. Improve secondary induction machinery, supports, service routing and material distinction later. The footprint is adjacent to elite activation/telegraph space and the new chamber; coordinate with elite ownership before changing it.

**CustodianCore — REFINE, implementation deferred.** Active `Chapter01CompleteConcept/Prefabs/CustodianCore.prefab`, 102 renderers / 259,354 triangles. Tall containment mass, radial cage/supports and orange central reactor survive the camera and establish final-arena hierarchy. The broad cylindrical wall, repeated pillars and luminous horizontal bands are too smooth and generic compared with the accepted chamber. The surrounding arena has scale but little functional service infrastructure. Retain the recognizable containment silhouette, clean boss telegraph lanes and encounter gate; refine wall thickness, platforms, access systems, credible conduit entries and purposeful machinery. Boss-owned actor/core/telegraph changes require a coordinated pass.

**ChapterServiceNetwork — REFINE overall; gate structures REBUILD.** Active `Chapter01CompleteConcept/Prefabs/ChapterServiceNetwork.prefab`, 45 renderers / 87,244 triangles. The connected route spine, side grates, trenches, plate fields and cross-route services provide useful topology and scale. The recurring flat deck rectangles, simple rail spans and generic pillars dominate at the lens; service routing lacks enough junction/access explanation. Preserve route continuity and necessary open combat floor, refine local floor/connection hierarchy. Its gate subassemblies need new primary structural forms rather than more boxes on the current lintels.

**IndustrialTransfer — REFINE.** Active `ConnectedConceptSectors/Prefabs/IndustrialTransfer.prefab`, 2 renderers / 54,092 triangles. Raised transfer/service connection between distribution and fabrication gives this group a coherent function and links the sectors physically. Thin spans, simplified support joints and cabinet ends appear generic when sharing the camera with the press and capacitors. Keep the functional bridge and route clearance; deepen load-bearing structure, cable/pipe entry logic and end connections. High triangle count in two combined meshes should be assessed by visible benefit before adding detail.

## Shared architecture families

**Gate/lintel and major transition frames — REBUILD.** Reviewed hub-to-relay, middle service junction, elite approach and boss approach in `transition_z-20`, `transition_z20`, `transition_z43` and `transition_z65`. Current posts and long rectangular overhead beams give clearance and direction but not credible gate machinery. Functional hinges/locks, load-bearing overhead depth, wall attachment and utility routing are absent or too schematic. Preserve the existing unlock/collision authority. Rebuild one isolated presentation frame first, then assess reuse; do not change every transition in the same pass.

**Large wall/housing and storage-cell family — REBUILD.** Present across hub, relay, shield, hauler and arena edges. Broad chamfered cuboids with vent strips and colored insets repeatedly become the main architecture. They have readable bulk and coarse external blockers but little variation in function, internal depth or service logic. Replace prominent primary wall masses with authored industrial assemblies; retain subordinate electrical cabinets only where size and purpose fit.

**Floor access, side grates and route rail vocabulary — KEEP as supporting parts; composition REFINE.** These elements communicate plate construction, maintenance paths and scale without obstructing the central fighting lane. The repetitive full-length deck pattern needs local functional variation and better transitions. Keeping an existing grate/rail does not approve the repeated flat floor composition as final Chapter architecture.

**Large supply pipes, cable spines and generator/diagnostic cabinet clusters — REFINE.** Their routes and association with sectors are useful. Strengthen meaningful endpoints, elbows, restraints, manifolds and service relationships; remove arbitrary freestanding clusters when rebuilding their owner facility. No fine pipe/bolt collider proliferation. Keep physical authority explicit and evaluate major cabinets with actual controller clearance.

**Arena infrastructure — REFINE, coordinated later.** The Magnetar/Custodian approaches and open fight surfaces are readable. Edge walls, service platforms, supply interfaces and containment perimeter need stronger physical purpose and material hierarchy. Gate progression, active encounter boundaries and telegraph lanes remain outside this branch's replacement scope.

## Ranked next target

1. **Repair Hub production replacement proof — `CHAPTER01_REPAIR_HUB_ARCHITECTURE_PROOF_V1`.** Visibly weak primary installation, seen at start and every repair return, central to Chapter identity, extensive box housing language, and lowest combat/actor ownership risk. Keep the spawn/repair interaction pocket and layout authority, replace one environment prefab, and compare the live portrait approach against this chamber.
2. **One major gate/transition presentation.** Repeated often and visibly primitive, with broad identity benefit. Start with a non-progression transition; unlocking gates add ownership risk. A single modular prototype is the appropriate initial scope.
3. **Relay Yard.** Prominent early repeated encounter and generic primary geometry; preserve Scout origin and combat clearance.
4. **Hauler Graveyard.** Strong mismatch from toy-like wreck geometry; isolate environment wrecks from the Carrier actor.
5. **Shield Dump.** Rebuild the armor-processing facility; preserve Warden work.
6. **Cutting Floor, then Capacitor Field refinements.** Their functions and main silhouettes already work, reducing replacement urgency.

MagnetarComplex, CustodianCore and arena infrastructure are deliberately outside this implementation ranking until elite/boss ownership is coordinated. Rank uses qualitative exposure and inspected weakness, not invented player telemetry. No additional replacement proof was implemented in this branch: the accepted chamber promotion, physical integration and full audit are the bounded pass.

## Conflict and acceptance limits

The Chapter scene patch adds one production prefab instance/root and one external collision-root reference on the visual environment. All existing serialized fields, G-0, actors and encounter/progression data remain unchanged. Layout data adds four route waypoints to bypass the chamber; existing blockers, surfaces, required destinations and strong-spot positions are preserved. Two presentation/world composition files gain the optional collision-root binding/adoption; no gameplay-domain, enemy/elite/boss, player, combat, progression, persistence or loot code changes. Remote branch divergence is recorded in the preservation receipt. The only ahead-of-main remote scene divergence found was the old `chatgpt/phase3-visual-world-baseline-dev` lineage: its divergent scene patch adds the legacy `_phase3EnemyVisualCatalog` binding to the composition root. That change was inspected and is outside this architecture pass; no composition-root field is overwritten here. Current actor/proof branches also leave the physical layout unchanged. Future work that modifies the same scene or world layout must reconcile these additions explicitly.

The accepted proof remains PR #77, unmerged. Its original assets and this promotion share GUIDs; a future integration of both branches must reconcile the path move and avoid duplicate GUIDs. Device FPS, heat and a new human playable-APK visual gate are not established by desktop captures or tests. Render/material counts are measurement, not approval to repeat the chamber's cost throughout the Chapter.
