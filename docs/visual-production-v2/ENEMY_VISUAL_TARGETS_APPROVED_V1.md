## Shared faction language

All three belong to one coherent hostile machine faction:

- serious modern sci-fi combat machines;
- dark graphite exposed mechanics;
- cool/pale metallic armor shells;
- localized red/orange hostile energy;
- layered armor over visible joints and actuators;
- aggressive hard-surface architecture;
- mechanical plausibility at joints/supports/attack sources;
- readable silhouette from portrait gameplay camera;
- no toy-like proportions;
- no low-detail box-mech final form;
- no humanoid soldier-in-armor read;
- no neon-everywhere treatment;
- no obvious untouched stock-asset identity after production adaptation.

Silhouette/function must distinguish roles before color.

## Scout

Role: fastest ordinary recon/pressure enemy; fragile alone, dangerous in groups.

Target read:
- low radial/spider-like body;
- thin fast articulated supports;
- compact central mass;
- narrow pointed forward forms;
- small localized red hostile core;
- lightest armor coverage;
- silhouette/motion imply speed and agility.

Reject if bulky, static/symmetrical, toy-like, obviously stock, or confused with Cutter.

## Cutter

Role: medium ordinary close-combat frontal-pressure enemy.

Target read:
- larger/heavier than Scout;
- forward-biased predator silhouette;
- conspicuous paired cutting/blade structures;
- stronger frontal armor;
- low aggressive stance;
- red energy concentrated near core/cutting assemblies;
- attack direction immediately readable from gameplay camera.

Reject if blades are decorative/small, it reads as Scout-with-more-armor, or donor/stock identity dominates.

## Magnetar Guard

Role: elite heavy area-control threat.

Target read:
- much heavier/wider than Scout/Cutter;
- broad low multi-support stance;
- major layered armor masses;
- contained orange/amber central energy focal point;
- visible heavy joints, pistons/support systems;
- strong elite silhouette before glow is considered;
- mechanically more complex, not an enlarged ordinary unit.

Reject if only scale/glow differentiates it, the core reads as a floating lamp, or heavy armor becomes toy-like/static.

## Required hierarchy

`Scout < Cutter << Magnetar`

Show the hierarchy through:
1. silhouette mass;
2. limb reach/support stance;
3. armor layering;
4. mechanical complexity;
5. core/energy containment;
6. emissive intensity as a supporting cue only.

## Production rule

Donors are mechanics/geometry/rig sources, not automatic final art. Selection must follow `docs/ASSET_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md`; old named donor candidates or PR numbers are historical evidence, not a current requirement.

Keep current gameplay/controller/socket/attack contracts unless the active task explicitly changes them, but rebuild/refine presentation as needed to reach the visual target.

## Human acceptance

Production approval is judged in the real playable runtime at the portrait gameplay camera and ultimately in the Android APK on device.

Beauty renders, Blender views, screenshots and automated audits are supporting evidence only. Do not stop a production pass for intermediate human approval unless the owner explicitly requests it.
