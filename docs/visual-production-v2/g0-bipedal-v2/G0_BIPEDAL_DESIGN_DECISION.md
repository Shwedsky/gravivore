## Purpose-built G-0 anatomy

- Long paired mechanical legs with narrow hips, angled shin rails, visible joints and compact split toes. Stance has exactly two supporting feet.
- Torso is a tapered split carapace surrounding an exposed structural waist; swept chest plates form a vertical cleft. No square military chest box.
- Small recessed **diamond/slit gravity cell** sits between chest plates. Cyan is concentrated at the cell, sensor and emitter rails; no giant circular eye/core.
- Sensor is a low blade on a mechanical neck carriage, with a thin cyan aperture, no human face/helmet anatomy.
- Shoulders use swept shield vanes over visible actuators, rather than round human pauldrons.
- Arms are open linkages ending in unequal purpose-built gravity tools: split emitter lance and compact capture jaw. No stock guns or human hands.
- Rear mass consists of a narrow spine and two swept containment fins, integrated into the torso rather than a stock backpack/tank.
- Graphite structure, cool titanium shell, restrained pale armor highlights, cyan identity and small copper service accents. No camo or toy palette.
