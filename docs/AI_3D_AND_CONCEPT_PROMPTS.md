# AI 3D and Concept Prompts — Visual Replacement Production V2

Primary visual authority: approved GRAVIVORE prototype board `Концепт GRAVIVORE: Мехи и окружение.png`.

These prompts are production aids. They must not override the visual bible. Every generated result requires human review against the prototype before modeling or integration.

General style block to append where useful:

> Dark industrial sci-fi, compact techno-organic machines, layered hard-surface armor over exposed mechanical joints, clear large/medium/small shape hierarchy, worn graphite and steel materials, restrained localized emissive energy, modern mobile-game readability from top-down/isometric portrait camera, no humanoid exosuit proportions, no toy-like forms, no primitive boxes, no excessive neon, no copied franchise design language.

## 1. G-0 turnaround

### 2D concept prompt
Create a production turnaround sheet for **G-0 Tier 0**, the player machine from GRAVIVORE. Low non-humanoid techno-organic combat machine, compact spider/crab-like four-support stance, wider than tall, bright circular cyan gravity core embedded in the center-front torso, pale cool-gray layered armor plates over dark graphite mechanical skeleton, articulated multi-segment legs with visible hinge/piston logic, small mechanical feet/claws, forward tapered blade/mandible weapon structures establishing facing direction, dense but readable rear machinery, no conventional head, no humanoid torso, no military exosuit anatomy. Show front, 3/4 front, side, rear and top views with consistent proportions. Preserve a clear central core from top-down gameplay angle. Armor must be layered with real gaps exposing mechanics, not one smooth shell. Add restrained wear on front edges, feet and blade surfaces. White/neutral studio background, orthographic presentation, clear silhouette, production-design clarity.

### Image-to-3D / text-to-3D base mesh prompt
Generate a clean hard-surface base mesh for a low four-support techno-organic combat machine. Compact body around a central circular energy-core cavity, layered outer armor plates, dark internal mechanical frame, four articulated multi-segment legs with explicit hinge axes and negative space, two forward tapered blade/mandible weapon structures, compact rear machinery. Non-humanoid, no cockpit, no face, no human pelvis, no tank turret. Prioritize large and medium forms, clean separable parts, mechanically plausible joints, symmetry only in macro structure, no tiny greeble noise, no textures required, watertight or clearly separated hard-surface parts suitable for manual retopology and rigging.

## 2. Scout

### 2D concept prompt
Create a small hostile GRAVIVORE Scout enemy. Low radial spider-like industrial robot, clearly smaller than G-0, compact central body with exposed glowing red core, six or four thin fast articulated legs, minimal armor, dark graphite/black mechanical surfaces with limited gray plating, agile predatory stance, no long blades, no shield, no humanoid features. Silhouette must read instantly from top-down portrait gameplay camera and remain distinct when shown as black shape only. Include front/side/top and a gameplay-angle view.

### 3D base mesh prompt
Generate a compact low spider-like mechanical enemy base mesh with a small central core housing, thin articulated legs, clear hinge segments, low center of mass, minimal armor, no humanoid anatomy, no weapon held in hands. Keep parts separable for rigid mechanical rigging. Preserve negative spaces between legs and body. Avoid excessive detail.

## 3. Cutter

### 2D concept prompt
Create a GRAVIVORE Cutter ordinary enemy: narrow front-heavy hostile machine, lower than G-0, strong asymmetrical or paired cutting-blade silhouette, dark/gray mechanical body with glowing red energy core and narrow weapon-root accents. Long tapered blades dominate the attack direction, body mass sits slightly rearward to balance them, articulated low legs/supports, aggressive lunging posture. Must be unmistakably different from the small radial Scout and the broad Warden. Show neutral and attack poses plus top-down gameplay-angle silhouette.

### 3D base mesh prompt
Generate a low mechanical striker with a compact dark body, central energy-core recess, articulated ground supports, one long and one shorter tapered cutting assembly or two clearly separated blade arms. Keep blade roots mechanically jointed and suitable for attack animation. No humanoid arms or legs, no floating blade decoration, no generic spherical body.

## 4. Magnetar Guard

### 2D concept prompt
Create the elite enemy **Magnetar Guard** for GRAVIVORE. Large multi-legged industrial guardian, approximately 1.5–1.9 times the perceived mass of G-0, broad planted stance, thick articulated supports, layered heavy armor with deep overlap, dense mechanical joint housings, dark graphite structure and armored shell with restrained amber/orange energy containment nodes. It must look significantly more advanced and dangerous than ordinary enemies without becoming a boss. Integrate attack machinery into limbs/body rather than hand-held guns. Include a single readable weak/focal energy assembly. Show neutral stance, attack telegraph pose, top-down gameplay view and silhouette comparison beside G-0.

### 3D base mesh prompt
Generate an elite multi-legged hard-surface guardian base mesh with broad stance, heavy layered armor, large joint housings, visible internal mechanical frame and one central energy containment assembly. Use separable articulated leg segments and mechanically plausible pivots. Macro silhouette first, no random greeble field. Avoid reusing the same body plan as a hauler or ordinary spider bot.

## 5. Custodian M-0

### 2D concept prompt
Create **Custodian M-0**, the Chapter 1 boss of GRAVIVORE. Massive arena-defining spider-like industrial guardian, roughly 3–4 times G-0’s perceived body mass with several times its limb span, huge central armored body suspended over many long articulated mechanical legs/arms, dominant circular red-orange core visible in the torso, deep dark/red layered armor, very large hinge housings and exposed mechanical systems. The silhouette must communicate boss scale before VFX. Design three readable attack postures: long limb sweep, targeted beam from a dedicated emitter/core assembly, and planted radial ground attack. Preserve clear negative space between giant limbs, no humanoid torso or face, no scaled-up ordinary enemy look. Show front, side, top-down gameplay view and scale comparison with G-0.

### 3D base mesh prompt
Generate a large mechanical spider/guardian base mesh for a game boss. Massive central armored shell around a circular core cavity, 6–8 long articulated limbs with clear root/upper/lower/terminal segments, heavy joint housings, integrated attack emitters, layered plates and exposed internal frame. Keep major components separate for rigging. Prioritize top-facing and silhouette geometry. No tiny surface noise, no organic flesh, no humanoid anatomy, no generic insect exoskeleton.

## 6. Industrial environment kit

### 2D concept prompt
Design a cohesive modular industrial environment kit for GRAVIVORE Chapter 1 based on the approved prototype: dark graphite structural metal, mid-gray machinery, warm orange practical lighting, selective cyan technology, layered worn floor plates, framed walls, reinforced mechanical gates, pipes, tanks, reactors, columns, barriers, maintenance consoles and industrial machine clusters. The world must feel dense and functional while leaving readable combat lanes. Show a modular kit sheet plus one 15x20m top-down gameplay composition demonstrating dense edges and open center. Avoid giant blank rectangles, random crate scattering, sterile clean sci-fi and excessive neon.

### 3D base mesh prompt
Generate modular hard-surface industrial kit base meshes: 2m/4m floor panels, framed wall bays, half-height barriers, structural columns, pipe segments, tanks, maintenance machine housings and gate-frame components. Use consistent scale, grid-friendly pivots, beveled large forms, recessed panels and secondary structural detail. No textures, no tiny greeble spam, no monolithic cube walls.

## 7. Reactor

### 2D concept prompt
Design a hero industrial reactor for GRAVIVORE. Tall cylindrical or multi-sided energy chamber, 3–5m visual height, dark structural frame, mid-gray armor housings, large warm orange glowing core chamber with visible containment rings, thick pipe/cable connections, maintenance access, heavy base and top machinery. It must function as a strong environment landmark from top-down gameplay camera and look like real facility equipment rather than fantasy crystal decoration. Include a cyan service-tech variant as secondary option without changing the industrial construction language.

### 3D base mesh prompt
Generate a modular hero reactor base mesh with heavy floor base, vertical central chamber, armored collars, external frame, pipe connection ports, maintenance panels and visible energy aperture. Keep large pieces separate for materials/animation if needed. Avoid overly thin sci-fi fins and ornamental neon strips.

## 8. Wall / gate family

### 2D concept prompt
Design a GRAVIVORE industrial wall and gate family. Walls are framed structural bays with recessed machinery, ribs, vents, pipe channels and layered panels. Gate is a larger mechanical landmark with thick side frames, split/retracting door panels, actuator housings, lock geometry, threshold track, orange hazard markings and restrained status lights. Show straight wall, corner, pipe-wall, half-height barrier, standard gate and reinforced boss/elite gate variants. Everything must share the same graphite/gray worn material language and avoid primitive rectangular blockout appearance.

### 3D base mesh prompt
Generate a grid-compatible wall/gate modular base kit. 2m/4m wall frames with recessed panels, structural ribs, corner module, half-height barrier, pipe-integrated variant and heavy mechanical gate frame with separate moving panels and actuator housings. Real thickness, clean pivots, repeatable connections, no texture detail baked into geometry.

## 9. Material sheet

### 2D concept prompt
Create a physically based material reference sheet for GRAVIVORE showing: dark graphite structural metal, cool pale player armor, mid-tone industrial machinery steel, exposed polished worn edges, burnt-orange hazard paint, warm orange reactor emission, cyan/blue player/service emission, red hostile energy and boss red-orange core treatment. Each swatch should include clean, moderately worn and heavily worn examples with realistic roughness differences, localized grime and heat damage. Industrial and premium, not glossy plastic, not uniformly dirty, no excessive neon.

### Texture/material-generation prompt
Create tileable or trim-compatible PBR material studies for dark industrial sci-fi machinery: graphite metal, cool gray coated armor, mid-tone steel, hazard-painted metal and localized worn-edge variation. Provide consistent base-color logic, roughness/metallic separation, subtle AO/grime and optional emissive masks. Avoid baked directional lighting, giant scratches, procedural noise everywhere and color variation that destroys material identity.

## 11. Review rule

Generated concept/base-mesh output is acceptable only if it preserves:
- prototype silhouette hierarchy;
- G-0 low multi-support anatomy and cyan core;
- ordinary enemy role contrast;
- elite/boss scale escalation;
- dark worn industrial material language;
- dense-but-readable environment composition.

A visually impressive result that breaks these rules is a reject, not a creative alternative.
