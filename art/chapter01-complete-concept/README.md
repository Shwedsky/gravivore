# Chapter 01 — six functional installations and service network

Original GRAVIVORE geometry and procedural physical surfaces. No new external
payloads or licensed vendor source were acquired for this pass.

Reproduce with `Tools/chapter01-complete-concept/prepare.py`, then Blender 5.2
`--background --python Tools/chapter01-complete-concept/author.py`, then
`enhance.py` and `gate_refine.py` from the same tools folder, followed by
Unity editor `Gravivore.Editor.VisualIntegration.Chapter01CompleteConceptBuilder.Build`.
The editable Blender source preserves individually named engineering components
before export consolidation. All coordinates are metres; Blender X/Y/Z maps to
Unity X/Z/Y. Models are installed with unit scale, never nonuniform fitting.

The facilities intentionally differ by structure and process:

- Repair Hub: annular work plates, hinged diagnostic arms, protected service bays.
- Relay Yard: open deployment mouth, charging shoes, assembly fixtures, antenna arrays.
- Shield Dump: broad shield-storage cassettes, transfer gantry, trolley and lifting clamp.
- Hauler Graveyard: open ruptured transport shells, exposed chassis, suspended cargo rig.
- Magnetar Complex: laminated radial coil, magnetic return arch, conversion supplies.
- Custodian Core: shielded vertical chamber, sacrificial baffles, crown restraints,
  monolithic containment supports and segmented boss apron.
- Service network: slab expansions, actual grated undercroft, cargo guide rails,
  utility risers and architectural gate cheeks.

Materials reuse the project-owned CF/CS vocabulary from the confirmed two-sector
delivery. Three new original PBR families add service paint, armor shells and heat
damaged steel; their maps are not baked illumination or photographic third-party art.
Prior CF donor licensing remains covered by the existing ThirdPartyNotices and
Capacitor Field provenance. The approved CF/Cutting assets remain byte-preserved.

Gameplay collision, spawn identities, encounters, repair, rewards and saves are
owned by the existing runtime and data. These art prefabs contain no colliders,
gameplay scripts, scene cameras or runtime lights.
